# GLOSSARY.md — Gymly

Canonical domain language. Use these terms exactly; avoid the listed synonyms.

- **Owner** — A gym owner. One Supabase Auth user. Owns one or more gyms. The only login role in v1.
- **Gym** — One physical gym location belonging to an Owner. All data is scoped to a gym.
- **Plan** — A membership price template: name + amount (₹) + duration in days. Example: "3 months / ₹3333". Not tied to any person.
- **Member** — A paying person registered at a gym. Identity = phone number; one phone = one member per gym. Has a name and an optional note.
- **Subscription** — One *stretch*: a snapshot of a plan's price and duration (`price`, `duration_days`), a start date, and — once it ends — an end date and a reason (`cancelled` or `plan_change`). Stored as a row in the `memberships` table. The plan cycle renews by itself, so one subscription spans several cycles and nothing is written when a cycle turns over. A member holds many subscriptions over time; the current one is the row with no end date. Every plan period it enters puts that period's price on the tab. A legacy row with no price bills nothing.
- **Plan cycle** — One *paid period* of a subscription: `duration_days` long from its start date (a 90-day plan starting 12 Sep runs to 10 Dec, and the next cycle starts 11 Dec). Cycles are labels, not rows: the plan renews by itself and nothing is written when one turns over. A **cycle boundary** is the first day of the next cycle, and it is the only day a plan change may land. Avoid "billing period", "billing cycle". Each period puts its own price on the tab when it starts. Not the same as an **instalment day**, which is monthly.
- **Plan end** — The last day of the plan period the member is in: a 90-day plan starting 12 Sep has its first period end on 10 Dec, its second on 10 Mar. The day the balance on his tab comes due, and the date every money line is dated to. Not the same as an **instalment day**, which is when money is asked for.
- **Instalment** — One month of a plan: `price × 30 ÷ duration days` — a plan of 30 days or fewer asks for its whole price at once. The unit money is asked for in, and the number the owner thinks in (`₹3,000 ÷ 3 months = ₹1,000`). Avoid "monthly fee", "EMI".
- **Instalment day** — The day an instalment is asked for: the subscription's start date — the first month is due the day he joins, he pays before he trains — and each monthly anniversary after it, so the last instalment lands at the end of the plan. What has been asked of a subscription by a given day is its instalments that have arrived, capped at the price of the plan periods it entered. Avoid "deadline".
- **Due bucket** — Where a member sits on the Home triage, read from what has been asked for rather than from an expiry date: **Overdue** (an instalment has arrived and his payments are behind it), **Due soon** (not overdue, the next instalment is within 7 days and not covered), **Active** (everything else — including a member who has paid his plan and is waiting for the next period to ask).
- **Pending amount** (`pending`) — What the plans on his tab cost minus what he has paid: the price of every plan period he has entered, or what an ended subscription settled for. Positive means he owes it; shown as `₹2,000 pending`. Use `pending`; avoid "balance", "outstanding", "arrears".
- **Advance** — A pending amount below zero: money paid past the price of the plans on his tab, held in his favour. Shown as `₹500 advance`. Use `advance`; avoid "credit", "overpayment".
- **Payment** — Money received at the desk, recorded as a row in the `payments` table (`amount` > 0, `paid_on`, optional note). Correctable: a wrong entry is edited or deleted with confirmation, because a typo is not payment history. There is no gateway; the row is the receipt.
- **Plan change** — A change to a different plan, applied at a cycle boundary: the in-force subscription ends on the day *before* it (its last day in force) with reason `plan_change`, and the new one starts on the boundary day. Both rows are written at once, so no day is ever billed twice, and until the boundary arrives the detail screen shows it as the queued change with Undo. A switch waits for the paid period to run out — a six-month plan switched in its first month lands six months in, never stranding months already paid for.
- **Cancellation** — The member stops. The **Last day he came** (owner-entered, defaults to today) becomes the subscription's end date with reason `cancelled`, and money stops accruing there: the subscription settles at the days it ran, never below the instalments already asked of it, so leaving releases the months he never entered but cannot un-owe the month he started. What he already owes stays owed and an advance stays. A member is **cancelled** when no subscription is in force today and the most recent one ended `cancelled`.
- **Reactivate** — Start a new subscription from today, re-taking the plan snapshot (price overridable) on the same running tab. Idle days between the last day he came and today are never billed, and the new subscription's first instalment is due at once, like any other.
- **Payable to <date>** — The owner-facing wording for what the tab settles for on a chosen day, shown with `<n> of <total> days`: `Payable to 21 Oct — ₹2,000`, `40 of 90 days`. Used in the cancel sheet's preview.
- **Start date correction** — Rewriting the in-force subscription's start date in place when the member's real start date differs from the day the record was entered. Not a plan change: a correction edits the in-force row, and it moves the accrual clock and the instalment days with it. No expiry is stored (ADR-0002, amended by ADR-0003).
- **Inquiry** — A walk-in / call asking about the gym, not yet a member. Name + phone required, optional note. Statuses: `new`, `contacted`, `joined`, `lost`. Converting an inquiry creates a member (and usually a first subscription) and marks the inquiry `joined`.
